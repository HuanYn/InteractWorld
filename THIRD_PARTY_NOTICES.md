## Selected generated Demo media

The files under `assets/demos/` are selected outputs generated by this project's
self-trained checkpoints, plus sampled posters and GIF previews. They are not
official ABot/Wan promotional videos or a redistribution of original dataset
episodes. The media manifest records the exact selection and repackaging.

The input images/text for the historical experiments and matched-control demos derive from
[ABot-World-Explorer-500h](https://huggingface.co/datasets/acvlab/ABot-World-Explorer-500h).
The [pinned dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/acvlab/ABot-World-Explorer-500h/blob/49118ecb23a069abdab522b3cbd1f3d0588d040c/README.md)
declares Apache-2.0. Existing rights in the underlying game imagery/content remain
with their respective holders; this repository does not claim ownership of them
or imply that the code license overrides third-party asset rights.

Clips retain their original generated video frames and HUD; container metadata
was removed without re-encoding the H.264 video. Old HUD layouts may differ from
the current left-WASD/right-arrow layout. Model weights, original dataset videos,
raw operational receipts, machine details and private logs are not distributed.
The clips include unsuccessful experiments and are not a real-time or
general-quality certification. Full details: [version history](docs/version-history.md)
and [media manifest](assets/demos/manifest.json).
